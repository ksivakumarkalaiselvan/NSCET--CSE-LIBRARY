📋 Google Sheets Database Setup Instructions

When you are ready to persist data to your own Google Sheets, follow these exact instructions:

1. Required Google Spreadsheet & Tabs:

Create a Google Spreadsheet named ScholarLib College Library Database with these 5 tabs:

  • Books (Columns: id, isbn, title, author, publisher, category, edition, publishedYear, totalCopies, availableCopies, rackNumber, coverUrl, description, status, createdAt)
  • Transactions (Columns: id, studentId, studentRegNo, studentName, bookId, bookTitle, issueDate, dueDate, returnDate, status, fineAmount, finePaid, issuedBy, notes)
  • Students (Columns: id, regNo, name, department, email, phone, status, passwordHash, createdAt)
  • Users (Columns: id, name, email, phone, role, status, passwordHash, createdAt)
  • Settings (Columns: key, value)

2. Complete Google Apps Script:

The ready-to-paste script is provided in google-sheets-integration/Code.gs in your project repository.

3. Deploy as Web App:

In Apps Script: Click Deploy > New Deployment > Web app. Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone. Copy the Web App URL and paste into GOOGLE_SHEETS_SCRIPT_URL in your .env file!
